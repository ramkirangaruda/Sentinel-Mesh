## Calibration (the long part)
- Resistor plan changed three times as parts changed: Ramkiran's 39/47/56 Ω plan → a ¼ W 5 % kit (no 2 W parts in the
  shop) → the kit's 220 Ω packet was missing → **final: only 270 Ω (15 bought, carbon film) and 1 kΩ (5 bought,
  metal film), ¼ W**. Every resistor sits alone across 5 V in parallel, ≤ 0.10 W each. Loads L1 1 kΩ, L2 1×270,
  L3 3×270, L4 5×270, L5 10×270, L6 13×270 (5 to 248 mA). Rule: a single ¼ W resistor across 5 V must be ≥ 220 Ω.
- **Session 1 (2026-10-21): trial, excluded.** Found that (a) the INA219's zero depends on its input voltage
  (−0.31 mA at 0 V, −0.20 mA at 5 V), (b) the VIN− jumper's breadboard contact is unreliable (two readings with no
  current at the sensor), (c) the black test lead was failing (it then broke, twice), which inflated low resistances.
- **Session 2 (2026-10-24): the calibration for the paper.** New leads (0.6 Ω, subtracted). Result:
  **INA219 = 1.0367 × reference − 0.428 mA**; after correction every load from 57 to 232 mA agrees within **0.6 %**;
  below 20 mA ±0.2 mA. Warm R = cold R (no heating drift). INA219 bus voltage reads 1.4–3.1 % above the meter (part
  is a ~0.3 Ω drop in the VIN− jumper and breadboard). **Which instrument is right is not settled**: check L1 and L6
  against a better meter before quoting absolute joules; ratios (C2) do not depend on it. Full table in `setup.md`.

## Practical lessons (save time next session)
- Breadboard: 400-point, rails = the two left columns (+ next to the red line, − next to the blue line), continuous
  top to bottom (checked). Resistors stand up with one leg bent back to span the adjacent +/− holes.
- Measure a load's resistance with the INA219 disconnected (or one resistor leg lifted); otherwise the INA219's
  input diodes and the adaptor show up as a direction-dependent reading (we saw 930 vs 150 Ω).
- Probe on resistor legs, not jammed into breadboard holes; male-male "test point" jumpers were too wobbly.
- After touching the VIN− jumper, check that M actually sees current before taking a reading.
- Keep the meter flat with leads straight; hold plugs, not cables.

## First energy results on the rig (2026-10-25, one run per condition, raw logs in `paper/data/E_rig/`)
F powered only through the INA219 (`field_node_energy`), G and A on USB, M logging; INA219 corrected with the
session-2 calibration. Analysis so far is a scratch quick-look; the paper's scripts are task S1-S3.

| Condition | F mean power, attack window | vs baseline | Legit handshakes in attack |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle, radio on (no attack) | ~603 mW (~118 mA at 5.06 V) | - | - |
| FLOOD 5/s, defence none | 605 mW | no change within noise | 3 ok / 4 fail |
| FLOOD 5/s, gate v2 | 606 mW | no change (602 challenged) | 4 ok / 0 fail |
| **INSIDER 5/s, defence none** | **705 mW** | **+104 mW (+17 %), +12.6 J in 121 s** | 2 ok / 6 fail |
| **INSIDER 5/s, gate v2** | 604 mW | no change (551 challenged) | 4 ok / 0 fail |

- A legitimate handshake costs F about 9-65 mJ above idle (80-560 ms; ML-DSA signing time varies a lot).
- **Finding 1, idle dominates:** with the radio always on, F idles at ~0.6 W. Any attack only matters relative to
  that; for a duty-cycled (sleeping) node the picture is very different. The paper must state the radio duty cycle.
- **Finding 2, FLOOD is cheap to reject:** FLOOD fills the signature with random bytes; ML-DSA verification rejects a
  badly formed signature in ~1.7 ms before the expensive maths, so FLOOD barely costs F energy. The comment in
  `firmware/src/attacker/main.cpp` ("one ML-DSA verification that fails") is wrong in practice.
  **Needed (task S5, Ramkiran):** a "well-formed forgery" mode: sign one message once with the attacker's key, then
  resend that correctly encoded signature with fresh random nonces under the gateway's identity. Each copy forces
  the full verification (fails only at the end) while the attacker pays nothing per HELLO. That is the attack the
  paper's asymmetry claim needs.
- **Finding 3:** INSIDER (correctly signed, enrolled-key) is costly: +17 % power with no defence. Gate v2 removed
  the extra drain completely and kept the real gateway connected (4/4). The ECHO-insider (copies cookies) is untested.
- All of this is n = 1 per condition: repeats (n >= 5) come from the E5 grid.
